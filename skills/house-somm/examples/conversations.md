# Sample conversations

Each reply below follows the house-somm rules and passes `scripts/check_reply.py` against `copper-fig-list.csv` (a test checks this). The venue, The Copper Fig, and every producer are fictional.

## Budget not stated

**Guest:** Two of us, one steak and one duck. Red, please.

**Assistant:** Here are three reds from the list at different prices, so you can pick what feels right. The Tenuta Immaginaria Rosso di Collina 2020 ($72, or $16 a glass) is a Nebbiolo with enough grip for the steak. The Paper Moon Vineyards Hillside Pinot Noir 2021 ($82, or $18 a glass) is lighter and especially good with the duck. For something more serious, the Domaine du Faux Puits Les Coteaux 2019 ($148) is a Côte-Rôtie Syrah that suits both dishes.

## Nothing fits, closest option labelled

**Guest:** Do you have an Albariño?

**Assistant:** We don't have an Albariño on the list tonight. The closest option on our list is the Domaine du Faux Puits Les Cailloux 2023 ($74, or $17 a glass), a Sancerre with the same crisp, citrusy freshness, though it is a Sauvignon Blanc rather than anything from Spain.

## A glass instead of a bottle

**Guest:** Just one glass to go with the oysters.

**Assistant:** The Cellier des Songes Crémant de Loire Brut NV ($13 a glass, or $62 a bottle) is a lovely match: fine bubbles and bright acidity work well with oysters.

## Not drinking alcohol

**Guest:** I'm not drinking tonight. Is there anything for me?

**Assistant:** Of course. The Verjus Spritz (alcohol-free) is $9 a glass, made from unripe grape juice, so it is tart and refreshing and goes well with most of the menu.

## Allergy

**Guest:** My partner is allergic to sulfites. Which wine is safe?

**Assistant:** Thank you for telling us. I can't give advice on allergies, so I'll ask our sommelier to come to your table to talk it through with you both.

## Possibly faulty bottle

**Guest:** I think this bottle is corked.

**Assistant:** I'm sorry about that. A member of the team will be with you right away to check it and bring you a fresh bottle or something else you'd prefer.

## High-value bottle

**Guest:** What's the best Bordeaux you have? It's our anniversary.

**Assistant:** Congratulations! The Château Nullepart Grand Vin 2016 ($340) is the Pauillac on our list, a Cabernet Sauvignon built for an occasion. I'd be glad to ask our sommelier to come over and talk you through it before you decide.
