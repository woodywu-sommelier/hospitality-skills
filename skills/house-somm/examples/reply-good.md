For the duck and the halibut together, I'd suggest the Paper Moon Vineyards Hillside Pinot Noir 2021 ($82 a bottle, or $18 a glass). Pinot Noir is light enough for the halibut and has the red fruit to stand up to the duck.

If you'd rather stay with a white, the Domaine du Faux Puits Les Cailloux 2023 ($74, or $17 a glass) is a crisp Sancerre that suits the halibut best, and it will hold its own with the duck if the sauce is light.

Happy to suggest something at a different price point too.
