# Privacy

Hospitality Skills does not collect, store or transmit data.

- Its scripts and its local MCP server read only the text or files passed to them.
- They write nothing to disk, make no network calls and keep nothing after they return, so there is nothing to retain or delete.
- The plugin does not ask for personal data and has no accounts, analytics or tracking.

When you use the plugin through Claude, Claude processes your conversation under your agreement with Anthropic. The plugin adds no other processing and sends your data to no other recipient.
